The steps

Meet. Design. Scope. Built fast.

  1. 1

    Meet

    A free discovery call about your business, your customers, and what you need. You talk directly to the person who will build it.

  2. 2

    Design

    We agree the look together: colors, fonts, layout, and feel. We revise with you until it’s right, within the agreed scope.

  3. 3

    Scope

    You get a written scope of work: every feature, the timeline, the price, and the payment terms. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  4. 4

    Build

    Work starts right away. You get progress updates and previews while it’s built.

  5. 5

    Launch & support

    We launch it, test it, and hand it over the way your plan calls for. Then a monthly support plan or pay per job.

Pricing, ownership, support

Two ways to work together.

One-time project

Pay once, it’s yours.

  • One price for the whole job, agreed in the scope
  • The code, domain, and accounts are yours
  • Support whenever you need it, paid per job
Monthly plan

We run it for you.

  • A simple monthly price, agreed in the scope
  • We host and manage it for you
  • Fixes, updates, and new features included
  • 3-month minimum, then month to month

Either way, the price, timeline, and payment terms are written into your scope before any work begins.
