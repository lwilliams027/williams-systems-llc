Solutions / Ongoing support

Launch day is the start, not the finish.

The same team that built it keeps it running, keeps it current, and keeps adding to it, on a monthly plan sized to what you actually need.

Get startedSee how we work

What you get

Someone who already knows your software.

  • The same people

    No handover to a support desk that has never seen your code.

  • Watched while you sleep

    Monitoring and alerts, so problems get found before your customers find them.

  • Kept current

    Updates and security patches applied as they come, not once a year.

  • Fixes

    Something broken gets looked at quickly, with a clear answer about what happened.

  • New features

    The list you have been adding to since launch, worked through a piece at a time.

  • Backups that are checked

    Taken automatically, and restored occasionally to prove they work.

Who it's for

For software that has to keep working.

Software is never finished. The question is whether the person maintaining it knows how it was built.

  • After we launch you

    The natural next step once your project goes live.

  • Software someone else built

    We can take it on, once we have reviewed it.

  • No developer of your own

    You need a team on call without hiring one.

How it works

One team, from first call to launch.

  • We meet

    A free call about what you need, what it is worth to you, and whether we are the right people for it.

  • We agree the look

    Colours, layout, and feel settled with you before anything is built, so the first version already looks like yours.

  • You get it in writing

    A scope of work covering what we are building, how long it takes, and the price: a one-time cost for the job or a monthly plan, whichever fits.

  • We build it, fast

    Work starts as soon as you sign, you see it as it goes, and the same team supports it afterwards.
