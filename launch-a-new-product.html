You have the idea. We build the thing.

Go from a sketch on a napkin to software your customers can sign up for. One team designs it, builds it, ships it, and keeps it running.

What you get

Everything a first version needs.

  • A design you approve first

    Real screens you can click through before a line of code is written, so nothing is a surprise.

  • The product itself

    Built custom for what you are actually doing, not bent out of a template.

  • Accounts and sign-in

    Users, passwords, permissions, and the boring security parts done properly.

  • Payments, if you sell

    Checkout, subscriptions, and receipts wired up and tested.

  • Somewhere to run it

    Hosting, domains, and backups set up in accounts you own.

  • A way to see how it is doing

    Simple analytics and alerts, so you know what people actually use.

Who it's for

For the first version, done right.

Most first versions fail slowly: built from a template, held together by three vendors, impossible to change. We build yours to be changed.

  • Founders

    You have customers waiting and need something real in front of them.

  • Established businesses

    A new product line that should not be bolted onto the old system.

  • Teams replacing a tool

    The software you rent does not fit, so you are building your own.

How it works

One team, from first call to launch.

  • We meet

    A free call about what you need, what it is worth to you, and whether we are the right people for it.

  • We agree the look

    Colours, layout, and feel settled with you before anything is built, so the first version already looks like yours.

  • You get it in writing

    A scope of work covering what we are building, how long it takes, and the price: a one-time cost for the job or a monthly plan, whichever fits.

  • We build it, fast

    Work starts as soon as you sign, you see it as it goes, and the same team supports it afterwards.

Questions

Launch a new product, answered.

Don't see your question? Ask us directly.

How small can a first version be?

Smaller than you think. We look for the shortest path to something people can genuinely use, then add from there once it is in their hands.

How long does it take?

We move fast and we tell you the timeline in writing before starting. Small products ship in weeks, not quarters.

What does it cost?

Either a one-time price for the job or a monthly plan, depending on what the work looks like. Whichever it is, it is agreed in the scope before anything begins.

Who owns what we build?

You do. The code, the accounts, the domain, all of it, from day one.
