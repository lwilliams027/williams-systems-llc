Questions

Mobile apps, answered.

Don't see your question? Ask us directly.

Do I need two separate apps for iPhone and Android?

No. We build one app from a single codebase that runs on both, which saves time and keeps features in sync.

Will you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We handle the store listings, screenshots, review process, and release, under your developer accounts.

Can the app use the same accounts as my website?

Yes. The app can share the same back end, so customers sign in once and see the same data everywhere.

How are updates handled after launch?

Many updates can be delivered instantly without a store review, and larger releases go through the stores as normal.
