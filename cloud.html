    How it works

    Meet. Design. Scope. Built fast.

    A simple process with no surprises. You know exactly what you're getting and what it costs before any work begins.

    1. Meet

      A quick call about what you run today, where it’s hosted, and what keeps you up at night.

    2. Design

      We plan the setup with you: hosting, deployments, monitoring, backups, and who can access what.

    3. Scope

      You get a written scope of work listing every feature, the price, and the timeline.

    4. Build

      Approve the scope and we start right away. We build fast and keep you updated until it's live.

    Clear, upfront pricing

    Some projects are a one-time price and some are a simple monthly plan, whichever fits your business. Either way, the price is set in your scope before we start, with no surprise invoices.

    Fast turnaround

    Work starts as soon as you approve the scope, and we move quickly, so you're up and running fast.

    Whose cloud accounts do you use?

    It depends on the plan. On a one-time setup, everything is in accounts you own. On a monthly plan, we can host and manage it for you. Either way, we document it all.

    Can you move us from our current host?

    Yes. We plan the migration, move data safely, and switch over with as little downtime as possible.

    Do you manage the infrastructure after setup?

    We can. Ongoing monitoring, updates, and support are available from the same team.

    Will this lower our hosting bill?

    Often, yes. Right-sizing resources and removing unused services usually brings costs down.
