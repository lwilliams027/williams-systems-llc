    What is a personalized AI?

    A personalized AI is an assistant built around a specific person or business. It is connected to your own documents, data, and tools, and set up to respond in your voice, so its answers and actions fit how you actually work.

    Is my data private?

    We set clear limits on what the assistant can see and do, and we talk through which AI provider and settings fit your needs before anything is connected.

    What can a personalized AI connect to?

    Most tools with an API, including email, calendars, CRMs, help desks, spreadsheets, databases, and your own software.

    Do I need technical knowledge to use it?

    No. You talk to it the way you would talk to a colleague, on your website, in your app, or in the chat tools you already use.
