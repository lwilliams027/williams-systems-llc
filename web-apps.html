    How it works

    Meet. Design. Scope. Built fast.

    A simple process with no surprises. You know exactly what you're getting and what it costs before any work begins.

    1. Meet

      A quick call about how your team works today: the spreadsheets, the manual steps, and what slows you down.

    2. Design

      We map out the screens and workflows with you: who uses it, what they need to see, and what happens next.

    3. Scope

      You get a written scope of work listing every feature, the price, and the timeline.

    4. Build

      Approve the scope and we start right away. We build fast and keep you updated until it's live.

    Clear, upfront pricing

    Some projects are a one-time price and some are a simple monthly plan, whichever fits your business. Either way, the price is set in your scope before we start, with no surprise invoices.

    Fast turnaround

    Work starts as soon as you approve the scope, and we move quickly, so you're up and running fast.

    Can you replace our spreadsheets with a web app?

    Yes. We start by learning how your team uses the spreadsheets today, then build a web app that keeps what works, removes the manual steps, and keeps your data in one safe place.

    Can it connect to the tools we already use?

    Usually, yes. Most modern tools have APIs, so the app can sync with payments, accounting, email, calendars, and CRMs.

    Who owns the web app?

    It depends on the plan. On a one-time project, the code, the domain, and the accounts are yours. On a monthly plan, we host and manage it for you. Either way, it’s written into your scope before we start.

    How secure is it?

    Security is built in from the start: encrypted connections, role-based access, audit logs, and regular backups.
