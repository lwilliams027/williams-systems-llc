    How it works

    Meet. Design. Scope. Built fast.

    A simple process with no surprises. You know exactly what you're getting and what it costs before any work begins.

    1. Meet

      A quick call where you walk us through the spreadsheet and how your team uses it.

    2. Design

      We turn your tabs and steps into screens, and agree the look with you.

    3. Scope

      You get a written scope of work listing every feature, the price, and the timeline.

    4. Build

      Approve the scope and we start right away. We build fast and keep you updated until it's live.

    Clear, upfront pricing

    Some projects are a one-time price and some are a simple monthly plan, whichever fits your business. Either way, the price is set in your scope before we start, with no surprise invoices.

    Fast turnaround

    Work starts as soon as you approve the scope, and we move quickly, so you're up and running fast.

    Do we lose the data already in our spreadsheets?

    No. We bring it across and check it with you before switching over.

    Can we still export to a spreadsheet?

    Yes. Plenty of people still want the numbers in a sheet, and that keeps working.

    How long before we can stop using the old file?

    It depends on the spreadsheet. We get the core of it working first so you can move off the sheet early, and the timeline is in your scope.

    What does it cost?

    A one-time price for the build, or a monthly plan if you would rather spread it and keep us on for changes. Agreed up front either way.
